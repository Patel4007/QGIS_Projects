# USA National Growth & Corridor Framework

Regional-scale USA project focused on metro hierarchy, corridor access, and zoomable planning zones across the lower 48.

## Included Files

- Project: `01_usa_national_growth_and_corridors.qgz`
- Data: `data/01_usa_national_growth_and_corridors.gpkg`
- Large-format PDF: `exports/01_usa_national_growth_and_corridors_layout.pdf`

## Notes

- The main map opens on the lower 48 so corridor and metro detail are readable immediately.
- Planning zones are analytical cells scored by proximity to major metros and corridor spines.
- Bookmarks jump directly to the Northeast Corridor, Great Lakes, Texas Triangle, and California.

## Data Basis

- Real country polygons from the offline Natural Earth low-resolution dataset bundled locally with the QGIS Python environment.
- Several indicator and corridor layers are analytical derivatives created for map design and comparison, not official observed datasets.
