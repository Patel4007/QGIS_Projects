# Asia Growth Gateways & Supply Chains

Continent-scale Asia project for zooming between East Asia, ASEAN, Gulf-India, and central overland systems.

## Included Files

- Project: `04_asia_growth_gateways_and_supply_chains.qgz`
- Data: `data/04_asia_growth_gateways_and_supply_chains.gpkg`
- Large-format PDF: `exports/04_asia_growth_gateways_and_supply_chains_layout.pdf`

## Notes

- The Asia project focuses on gateway structure rather than only country statistics.
- Supply chain zones are analytical cells scored from corridor reach, gateway clustering, and country pressure metrics.
- Seven bookmarks are tuned to East Asia, ASEAN, Gulf to India, Central Asia, the China coast, and the wider Indian Ocean frame.

## Data Basis

- Real country polygons from the offline Natural Earth low-resolution dataset bundled locally with the QGIS Python environment.
- Several indicator and corridor layers are analytical derivatives created for map design and comparison, not official observed datasets.
