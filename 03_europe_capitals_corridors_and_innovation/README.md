# Europe Capitals, Corridors & Innovation Arc

Continent-scale Europe project designed for zooming into corridor-rich capital regions and comparative innovation belts.

## Included Files

- Project: `03_europe_capitals_corridors_and_innovation.qgz`
- Data: `data/03_europe_capitals_corridors_and_innovation.gpkg`
- Large-format PDF: `exports/03_europe_capitals_corridors_and_innovation_layout.pdf`

## Notes

- The European opening extent is tighter than the world project so city and corridor detail reads immediately.
- Integration zones are clipped analytical cells driven by hub access, corridor reach, and country strength indicators.
- Seven bookmarks target North Sea, Rhine Alpine, Central Europe, Mediterranean, Iberia, and Baltic-Nordic frames for fast review.

## Data Basis

- Real country polygons from the offline Natural Earth low-resolution dataset bundled locally with the QGIS Python environment.
- Several indicator and corridor layers are analytical derivatives created for map design and comparison, not official observed datasets.
