# Middle East Energy, Water & Logistics Systems

Advanced Middle East project covering the East Mediterranean, Gulf, Red Sea, and plateau systems through corridor, hub, and interface analysis.

## Included Files

- Project: `07_middle_east_energy_water_and_logistics.qgz`
- Data: `data/07_middle_east_energy_water_and_logistics.gpkg`
- Large-format PDF: `exports/07_middle_east_energy_water_and_logistics_layout.pdf`

## Notes

- This project emphasizes strategic infrastructure structure rather than only country choropleths.
- Interface zones combine logistics reach, energy-transition capacity, strategic influence, and climate exposure.
- Bookmarks target the Eastern Mediterranean, the Gulf core, Mesopotamia and Iran, and the Red Sea frame.

## Data Basis

- Real country polygons from the offline Natural Earth low-resolution dataset bundled locally with the QGIS Python environment.
- Several indicator and corridor layers are analytical derivatives created for map design and comparison, not official observed datasets.
